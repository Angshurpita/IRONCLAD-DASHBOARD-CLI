# Class 10th Mathematics: Curriculum Standards & Knowledge Base

## 1. Real Numbers
Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic states that every composite number can be uniquely expressed (factorised) as a product of prime powers, apart from the order in which the prime factors occur:
$$n = p_1^{a_1} \cdot p_2^{a_2} \cdots p_k^{a_k}$$
For any two positive integers $a$ and $b$, the product of their Highest Common Factor (HCF) and Lowest Common Multiple (LCM) equals their product:
$$\text{HCF}(a, b) \times \text{LCM}(a, b) = a \times b$$
Irrationality proofs for $\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{3}, \sqrt{5}$ proceed by contradiction using divisibility lemmas.

## 2. Polynomials & Quadratic Equations
For quadratic polynomial $p(x) = ax^2 + bx + c$ ($a \neq 0$) with roots $\alpha$ and $\beta$:
- Sum of zeros: $\alpha + \beta = -\frac{b}{a}$
- Product of zeros: $\alpha \beta = \frac{c}{a}$
The roots of quadratic equation $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$ are given by the Quadratic Formula:
$$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{D}}{2a}, \quad D = b^2 - 4ac$$
The Discriminant $D$ determines root nature:
1. $D > 0$: Two distinct real roots
2. $D = 0$: Two equal real roots
3. $D < 0$: No real roots (complex conjugate roots)

## 3. Triangles & Geometric Theorems
Basic Proportionality Theorem (Thales' Theorem): If a line is drawn parallel to one side of a triangle intersecting the other two sides in distinct points, then the other two sides are divided in the same ratio:
$$\frac{AD}{DB} = \frac{AE}{EC}$$
Criteria for similarity of triangles:
- AAA (Angle-Angle-Angle) or AA criterion
- SSS (Side-Side-Side) proportional ratios
- SAS (Side-Angle-Side) ratio and included angle

## 4. Trigonometry & Standard Identities
For acute angle $\theta$ in right-angled triangle $ABC$ with right angle at $B$:
$$\sin \theta = \frac{\text{Opposite}}{\text{Hypotenuse}}, \quad \cos \theta = \frac{\text{Adjacent}}{\text{Hypotenuse}}, \quad \tan \theta = \frac{\sin \theta}{\cos \theta}$$
Standard Pythagorean identities:
$$\sin^2 \theta + \cos^2 \theta = 1, \quad 1 + \tan^2 \theta = \sec^2 \theta, \quad 1 + \cot^2 \theta = \text{cosec}^2 \theta$$

## 5. Statistics & Probability
For grouped frequency distributions:
- Direct Mean: $\bar{x} = \frac{\sum f_i x_i}{\sum f_i}$
- Modal class formula for Mode:
$$\text{Mode} = l + \left( \frac{f_1 - f_0}{2f_1 - f_0 - f_2} \right) \times h$$
- Median class formula:
$$\text{Median} = l + \left( \frac{\frac{n}{2} - cf}{f} \right) \times h$$
- Empirical relationship between measures of central tendency:
$$3 \times \text{Median} = \text{Mode} + 2 \times \text{Mean}$$
- Classical probability of event $E$: $P(E) = \frac{n(E)}{n(S)}$, with $0 \le P(E) \le 1$ and $P(E) + P(\bar{E}) = 1$.
